Sri Lankan World Heritage Sites

Sri Lanka is a beautiful island rich in history, culture, and natural beauty. It is home to several UNESCO World Heritage Sites that reflect the country's ancient civilization and unique heritage. These sites include ancient cities, sacred temples, historic forts, and beautiful natural landscapes. They attract visitors from all around the world and play an important role in preserving Sri Lanka's cultural and natural treasures for future generations.
